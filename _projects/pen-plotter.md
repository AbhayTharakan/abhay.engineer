A 2-axis pen plotter that traces vector paths with coordinated motion control —
every part designed from scratch, printed, and assembled.

- Designed the complete mechanical system in Fusion 360; every part modeled,
  3D printed, and assembled in-house.
- Handled dimensional planning and fit across the full assembly, tolerancing
  printed parts and mounts so the motion mechanism, pen carriage, and electronics
  integrated cleanly.

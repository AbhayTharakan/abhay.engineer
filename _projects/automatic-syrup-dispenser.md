An Arduino-controlled syrup dispenser built for a local business, where the
mechanical design had to package the electronics and bottles into an enclosure
that stayed serviceable in daily use.

- Designed the enclosure and a bottle-mounting system in Siemens NX, packaging
  the electronic components to measured specifications to guarantee fit and
  integration.
- Worked the mechanical design around the Arduino control hardware so the
  dispensing mechanism, wiring, and housing came together as one assembly.

A browser-based 3D digital-twin platform where you drag and drop industrial
machines onto a floor, wire them into production lines, and watch simulated
sensor data stream in real time.

- Architected the full drag-and-drop floor editor and line-wiring model, with
  live sensor telemetry rendered in the browser.
- Modeled per-machine sensor signals with stochastic processes — Ornstein-Uhlenbeck
  noise and thermal-lag dynamics — tuned to real datasheet specifications.
- Built a flow engine with discrete-unit buffers and bottleneck/starvation
  mechanics so line throughput behaves the way a real floor does.

A scale replica of the Neil Armstrong statue, reverse-engineered from a LiDAR
scan and paired with an Arduino-driven interactive quiz built into its enclosure.

- Modeled the statue in SolidWorks by reverse-engineering a LiDAR scan, producing
  print-ready geometry for both the model and its enclosure.
- Designed and wired an Arduino-driven quiz system with tactile button inputs,
  red/green LED feedback, and audio playback via a DFPlayer and speaker amplifier
  circuit.
